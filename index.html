HOW TO PLAY

Build a factory that runs, then build it deeper. Working machines add up to active engineering volume, and volume buys precision: higher layers, finer models, deeper research. At the bottom of the world is the material for a transmitter. Something out there answers, but only with the mechanisms it has heard you use.

Layers above yours turn to ghosts. Anything above layer 0 stands on a hollow frame; you can also build inside frames and walk under them.

Getting started

You land with 8 iron plates, a burner drill and a stone furnace. Mine coal and stone by hand, put the drill on iron ore facing the furnace, and fuel both. Craft from there (E). Electricity needs a steam generator plus small poles; machines only run inside a pole's blue supply area.

Reading the arrows

Hover or hold a building to see where things go. Blue: where an inserter picks up, or where one can feed a machine. Orange: where things come out. Green: something is feeding it. Red ✕: that won't work. Belts never load furnaces or assemblers directly: put an inserter between them. Drills push straight into whatever is in front. Alt hides the small arrows.

Logistics

Belts carry four items per tile and compress. Feeding a belt into the side of another merges onto it; a single side feed turns it into a curve. Inserters only take what the machine in front of them needs. Drills push straight into whatever is in front of them, or drop ore on the ground. Electric machines need a pole nearby; drag a belt over another to change it.
